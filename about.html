About FuseZap

Great chips deserve
great software.

FuseZap is a Techyodl product. We build the layer that turns new AI silicon into something customers can use on day one.

The hardest part of building an AI chip is no longer the silicon. It’s everything between the chip and the people who want to run models on it.

Customers expect the latest open models, the APIs they already use, and results they can trust. Delivering that takes time chip makers would rather spend on their hardware.

That’s the gap FuseZap fills: a complete, production-grade inference experience on top of the stack a chip maker already has.
