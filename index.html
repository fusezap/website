Questions chip makers ask us

Straight answers.

Do you replace our compiler or runtime?

No. FuseZap sits on top of what you’ve built and uses it. Your team keeps owning the parts that make your chip special.

How long does it take to get a model running?

It depends on how mature your toolchain is. Our goal is weeks, not quarters, for a first model. After a first call, we’ll give you an honest estimate.

Who owns the code for our chip?

You do. The code written for your chip is yours, and we can hand it over in full, with its tests, so your team can run it without us.

How do you protect our IP?

We work under NDA, on your hardware or in your environment if you prefer. Nothing about your chip goes into work we do for anyone else.

How do you prove it’s right?

Every result is checked against a reference implementation. You get a report with matching outputs and the accuracy of each number format, which you can share with your customers.

What does it cost?

Right now we’re working with a few design partners free of charge. After that: a fixed price to bring a chip up, and a subscription to keep it current, well below the cost of an in-house team.
